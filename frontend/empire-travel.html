About the Trip

Empire Lifestyle Travels invites you on a spectacular full-day escorted tour along the legendary Panorama Route one of South Africa's most breathtaking scenic drives through the Mpumalanga highlands.

From the awe-inspiring Three Rondavels viewpoint to the dramatic plunge pools of Bourke's Luck Potholes, the panoramic wonder of God's Window, an exhilarating descent on the Graskop Gorge Lift Co, and the serene beauty of Lone Creek Falls this is a day trip you will never forget.

All transport, entrance fees and refreshments are included. Simply show up and enjoy Empire Lifestyle Travels takes care of everything.

What's Included

  • Round trip transport (Nelspruit Engen Tiger Wheel & White River Crossing)
  • Three Rondavels viewpoint
  • Bourke's Luck Potholes
  • God's Window
  • Graskop Gorge Lift Co
  • Lone Creek Falls
  • Refreshments
