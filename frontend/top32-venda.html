About the Tournament

The TOP 32 Tournament Venda 2026 is a structured football movement dedicated to transforming lives through the beautiful game giving young, talented players from across the Vhembe District the exposure they deserve in front of scouts, coaches, and football stakeholders.

Hosted at Thohoyandou CTM Stadium and proudly backed by TS Galaxy FC Nedbank Cup champions and a professional PSL club this tournament is far more than a football competition. It is a platform for futures to be built, careers to be launched, and a community to be united.

Organised by the Ramarumo Azwinndini Divine Foundation, the event brings 32 teams and over 500 young players together for 10 days of professional football with full media coverage, production, and community engagement free for all spectators.
