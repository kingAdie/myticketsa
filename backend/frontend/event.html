About the Tournament

The Top 32 Tournament is an elite football competition bringing together the finest 32 teams from across South Africa for a knockout championship that celebrates skill, passion and the beautiful game.

Staged in the cultural heartland of Venda, Limpopo, and proudly supported by TS Galaxy FC, this tournament is more than a competition — it's a landmark celebration of South African football talent, community, and heritage.

Organised by the Ramarumo Azwinndini Divine Foundation, the tournament aims to spotlight emerging talent, promote youth development in sport, and bring South African communities together through the universal language of football.
